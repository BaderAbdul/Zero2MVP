## Value Proposition
**Build, Don't Study.**
This is not a traditional coding bootcamp or a simple AI tools tutorial. This is a product-building experience. You won't just learn about the latest AI tech—you will use it to accelerate your journey from a raw idea to a deployed application.

## Who It's For
For aspiring founders, product thinkers, and builders who are tired of being stuck in the "idea phase." If you have a problem you want to solve but previously lacked the technical velocity to build it, this program is for you.

## What You'll Build
You will move rapidly through the product lifecycle:
**IDEA → PRODUCT → PROTOTYPE → MVP → LIVE PRODUCT → PITCH**

## The 5-Level Journey
Experience a guided, gamified journey from nothing to a live startup pitch:
1. **🎯 Problem Hunter:** Discover and validate a real problem.
2. **📐 Product Architect:** Design the core flow and ship your first shell.
3. **⚙️ AI Builder:** Build the functional prototype using AI assistants.
4. **🌐 MVP Creator:** Connect your product to a real database.
5. **🏆 Founder:** Pitch your live, functioning product to the world.

## What You Leave With
By the end of the 3 days, you will walk away with:
- A live digital product deployed on the internet.
- A working core data loop (Auth & Database).
- A pitch-ready startup story.
- The confidence to build your next product using Agent-First methodologies.

We use industry-leading tools as our building engine:
- **BUILD:** Antigravity (Frontend and Logic)
- **CONNECT:** Firebase (Database and Authentication)
- **SHIP:** Vercel (Deployment)

## Demo Day
The climax of your journey. You'll have exactly 3 minutes to pitch your problem, demonstrate your live product, and convince the judges. We judge on execution, core flows, and solving real problems—not just pretty designs.

## The Experience
Track your progress on the Builder Leaderboard and earn your badges as you clear mentor checkpoints. When things get tough, jump into the Rescue Lane—our mentors provide Starter Kits (Accelerators) to ensure no team gets stuck for more than 15 minutes.

## FAQ
**Do I need to be a senior developer?**
No. You need to be a product thinker. Our Agent-First methodology and mentors will help you bridge the technical gap.

**Are we just learning to use Firebase or Vercel?**
No. The tools are just the engine. We are teaching you how to ship products quickly.

**What happens if we get stuck?**
We enforce the 15-minute rule. If you're stuck on a bug for 15 minutes, mentors will deploy an Accelerator to unblock you immediately.
