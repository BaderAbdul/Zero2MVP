# Demo Day & Judging

Demo Day judging is strictly aligned with the program philosophy: **SHIP UGLY**. We prioritize functional core loops that solve real problems over superficial visual polish.

## ⏱️ The 3-Minute Pitch Structure
Each team is strictly allocated 3 minutes to present, followed by 1 minute of Q&A.
- **00:00 - 00:30 | The Problem:** Who has it and why does it matter?
- **00:30 - 01:15 | The Solution:** How does this MVP solve the problem?
- **01:15 - 02:15 | Live Demo:** Show the product working. No vaporware.
- **02:15 - 02:45 | Why it Matters:** The core value proposition.
- **02:45 - 03:00 | What's Next:** Future vision.
- **+ 01:00 Minute | Q&A:** One question from the judges.

## 📊 Judging Rubric
Judges must evaluate based on these exact weights. Generic "UX" or purely visual scoring is NOT permitted.

| Criteria | Weight | Guidance for Judges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Problem** | 20% | Is the problem real, clearly defined, and validated? |
| **Core User Flow** | 15% | Is the primary user journey logical, intuitive, and focused? |
| **MVP Execution** | 25% | Does the product actually work? Did they successfully build and connect the core loop? |
| **AI Integration** | 20% | Did they use AI meaningfully in the product or in their building process? |
| **Pitch** | 20% | Was the presentation clear, convincing, and delivered within the time limit? |

## ⚠️ Important Rules for Judges
- **Starter Kits:** Teams are encouraged to use Starter Kits (Accelerators). There is **ZERO** penalty for using a Starter Kit.
- **Ship Ugly:** A functioning, connected app that looks basic should score higher in MVP Execution than a beautiful app that doesn't actually persist data.
- **Technical Failure Protocol:** If a live demo fails, the team can pivot to a pre-recorded video if available, or explain the core flow. They will lose points in MVP Execution, but can still score well in Problem and Pitch.

## 🏆 Awards
- **Overall Winner (Best MVP):** Highest total score on the rubric.
- **Best Problem Hunter:** Highest score in the Problem category.
- **Best Core Flow:** Highest score in the Core User Flow category.
- **Best AI Integration:** Highest score in the AI Integration category.
- **Best Pitch:** Highest score in the Pitch category.
- **Audience Choice:** Voted on by all attendees.

## Tie-Breaking
In the event of a tie, the team with the highest score in **MVP Execution** wins.
